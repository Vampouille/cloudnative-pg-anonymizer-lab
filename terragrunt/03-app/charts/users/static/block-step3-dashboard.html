Goal: Access the graphical Kubernetes Dashboard to visualize your resources.
1. Copy your service account token

Use the Copy Token button in the toolbar to display and copy your token, then paste it on the dashboard login page.

2. Open the dashboard

You will be prompted for your credentials: __USER__ / __PASSWORD__.

▶ Open Kubernetes Dashboard
Tip: In the top-left namespace selector, choose __USER__ to scope the view to your resources only.
